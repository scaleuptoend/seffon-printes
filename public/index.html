About Us

Established in 2012, Seffon Dupatta Centre is a wholesale manufacturer and supplier of exclusive fancy dupattas, hijabs, scarfs and stoles. Over the years we have built a strong reputation among retailers, boutiques and resellers for consistent quality, a wide choice of colours and fabrics, and competitive pricing.

We work exclusively with the trade. Every order, from a single bulk lot to regular repeat supply, is handled with the same commitment to quality, timely dispatch and transparent dealing. With branches in Chennai and Surat, we deliver to businesses all over India.

Resellers are always welcome. Contact us for wholesale rates, catalogues and bulk order enquiries.
